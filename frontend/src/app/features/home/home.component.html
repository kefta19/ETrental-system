Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about renting on ETrental

How do I search for a rental property?

Use the search bar to enter a location, property type or budget. You can also browse by popular locations and rental types to quickly narrow down your options.

Is it free to contact landlords?

Yes. Creating an account and contacting landlords through messages or appointment requests is completely free for renters.

How do I list my property?

Click "List your property", add your details and photos, and publish. Your listing reaches thousands of renters searching every month.

How do I schedule a viewing?

Open any property you like and request an appointment. The landlord will confirm a time that works for both of you.
